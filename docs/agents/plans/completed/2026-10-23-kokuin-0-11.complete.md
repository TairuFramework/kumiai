# kokuin controller 0.2 adoption (band 0.11) — complete

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Status:** complete
**Packages:** all fourteen (band 0.11). `@kumiai/mls` carries the behaviour change; the other thirteen
move dependencies and rise with the band.
**Origin:** kokuin shipped a management tier on the controller log (controller 0.2, capability 0.5,
token 0.6). kumiai sat a minor behind on all three and nothing auto-upgraded across a 0.x caret.

## Key decisions

- **Adapter at the kokuin boundary, formats unchanged.** Leaf `controller.prefix`, ledger `proof` and
  `recordedLog` stay `Array<SignedEvent>` (the root chain). `packages/mls/src/controller-log.ts` wraps
  them as `{ chain, events: [] }`; every fold goes through `foldChain`, and the embedded resolver's
  `loadLog` uses the same wrapper.
- **A device revoke is a deny-gaining rotate.** controller 0.2 removed the chain `rev` event. A revoke
  is now a same-generation root rotate whose snapshot deny set gains the subject (`revokesSubject`).
  Resets are excluded: a snapshot-bearing reset may carry a deny set and must not prove a revoke.
  Every revoke therefore needs the root seed and adds a chain event.
- **Any chain `rev` is refused.** kokuin 0.2 skips an unsigned `crit: false` event without checking a
  signature, so a chain holding a `rev` (any `crit`, signed or not) does not fold
  (`CHAIN_REVOKE_REFUSED`).
- **Member-issued grants fail closed.** The embedded resolver forwards an allowlist (`method`,
  `resolve`, `resolveHistoric`, `resolveAgreementKey`, its own `resolveDenySet`), so `resolveMember`
  and `frozen` never reach a verifier; `assertControllerGrant` already requires `iss === sub`.
  The management tier was adopted in 0.12: `2026-10-24-management-tier.complete.md`.
- **No pre-0.11 data support (user decision).** Only internal stack consumers exist. 0.1-format logs
  (non-snapshot rotates, chain `rev`) no longer fold; old lifecycle groups are recreated, and 0.10
  and 0.11 peers cannot share one. Three review rounds showed that partial compatibility leaks,
  because 0.2 cannot authenticate 0.1 data; the shims were removed.

  mutates the handle.

  first test had paid that transform inside its 5s timeout.
- Changesets `kokuin-0-2.md` (mls) and `band-align-0-11.md` (the other thirteen).
